# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful and technically rigorous
community. Participants must communicate in good faith, respect privacy and
licenses, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and
deliberate disruption.

Report a safety or conduct concern privately through the security contact in
`SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or take
other proportionate action when this policy is violated. Reports are handled
confidentially where possible.

This policy applies to the repository, its issue and pull-request spaces, and
project-related community channels.
